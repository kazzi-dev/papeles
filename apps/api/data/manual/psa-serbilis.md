# PSA: ordering birth, marriage and death certificates, CENOMAR and CENODEATH online

Source: https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/
Last checked: 2026-10-26

PSA Serbilis is the Philippine Statistics Authority's online service for requesting civil
registry documents, with delivery anywhere in the Philippines or abroad.

## Documents you can order

- Birth certificate
- Marriage certificate
- Death certificate
- CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage Record)
- CENODEATH (Certificate of No Death Record)

## Whose documents you may order

- Birth certificates: your own, your child's, your parents', and your spouse's (if you are
  married or widowed).
- Marriage certificates: your own, your parents', and your spouse's (if married or widowed).
- Death certificates: your parents', your child's, and your spouse's (if married or widowed).
- CENOMAR or CENODEATH: your own, your child's, your parents', and your spouse's (if married
  or widowed).
- Before choosing the certificate, you must enter your own personal information: sex, civil
  status, date of birth and your parents' names.

## Ordering a printed certificate for delivery (the main online service)

1. Request the certificate online on psaserbilis.com.ph.
2. Make the payment.
3. Wait for delivery. No visit to a PSA outlet is needed for this service.

The fee for a delivered printed certificate is not stated on the PSA Serbilis home page; the
site shows it during the request.

## Delivery of printed certificates

- Deliveries are handled only by PSA Serbilis' authorized couriers: LBC Express within the
  Philippines and DHL Express for customers abroad.
- Metro Manila and nearby provinces: "next day" delivery once PSA finishes processing and
  releasing the document.
- Other provinces: 6 to 8 working days. Add 5 to 7 working days for areas outside the
  courier's standard serviceable areas.

## Viewable online copy (e-copy): a separate service, not a delivered certificate

- This is different from ordering a printed certificate for delivery. You can request a viewable online copy of a PSA certificate, viewable for 60 days through an
  access code.
- Application fee: PHP 130.00 for birth, marriage or death certificates; PHP 185.00 for
  CENOMAR or CENODEATH. Payable at any PSA CRS outlet.
- Personal appearance at a PSA CRS outlet is required. Viewable online requests paid online or
  through other channels (not at a PSA CRS outlet) will not be processed.
- Within the 60-day validity you can get a printed copy on security paper at any PSA CRS
  outlet through the DocPrint service.

## If PSA advised you to go through endorsement

You can fill out an Electronic Endorsement Request Form online for free, print it, and present
it to the concerned Local Civil Registry Office together with the Negative Certification from
PSA.
