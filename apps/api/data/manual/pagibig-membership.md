# Pag-IBIG Fund: membership registration, MID number, and updating records

Source: https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/member_membership.html
Last checked: 2026-10-26

## Registering as a new member

- New members sign up online through Virtual Pag-IBIG.
- After registering you receive a Registration Tracking Number (RTN).

## Verifying your permanent MID number

- Two (2) to three (3) working days after you receive your RTN, go to Virtual Pag-IBIG to
  verify your Membership ID (MID) number.

## If you forgot your MID number

You can retrieve it in any of these ways:
- Through the official Pag-IBIG Fund Facebook page.
- By emailing the Contact Center at contactus@pagibigfund.gov.ph and asking for retrieval of
  your MID number.
- At any Pag-IBIG branch, bringing one (1) valid ID for verification.

## Changing or updating your information

- Visit any Pag-IBIG branch and submit the Member's Change of Information Form with the
  supporting documents.
- Updates covered: change of membership category; correction of name, birth date, marital
  status, place of birth, mother's maiden name, or sex; change of address or contact details;
  change of employment details; updating of heirs.
- Whenever photocopies are submitted, the original or a certified true copy must be presented
  for authentication.
- Documents issued abroad (for example birth, marriage or death certificates) must be
  apostilled if the issuing country is a member of the Hague Apostille Convention; otherwise
  they must be certified by the Philippine Embassy or Consulate General in that country.

## Merging or consolidating records (if you have more than one Pag-IBIG record)

Requirements:
- Accomplished Request for Consolidation/Merging of Member's Records (RCMMR)
- One (1) valid ID
- One (1) photocopy of your SSS Employment History

Visit any Pag-IBIG branch and submit the RCMMR with these documents.
