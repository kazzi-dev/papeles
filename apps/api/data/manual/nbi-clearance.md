# NBI clearance: online registration, renewal, and free clearance for first-time jobseekers

Source: https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph/
Last checked: 2026-10-26

NBI clearances are applied for online through NBI Clearance Online Registration and
Application Services (NBI Clearance eServices) at clearance.nbi.gov.ph.

## Creating an account

- Register a new account on clearance.nbi.gov.ph.
- When registering you may optionally register using a government ID, or using a previous NBI
  clearance (issued 2014 onwards).
- The form asks for: sex, civil status, birth date, first, middle and last name, mobile number,
  an active email address, and a password. You must read and accept the Terms of Service.

## Online renewal with delivery

- If your NBI clearance is expired or expiring, you can renew it online and have it delivered
  door-to-door. This applies to clearances from October 16, 2016 onwards.

## First-time jobseekers

- The portal has a separate service for first-time jobseekers, in compliance with Republic Act
  No. 11261, the "First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act".

## Checking a clearance

- NBI Clearance Online Verification lets you check the authenticity and validity of an NBI
  clearance.

Not covered here: the list of accepted valid IDs, fees, and appointment steps. The portal shows
these during application; check clearance.nbi.gov.ph for the current list.
