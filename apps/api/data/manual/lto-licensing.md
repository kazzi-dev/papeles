# LTO: student-driver's permit, new driver's license, renewal, and fees

Source: https://portal.lto.gov.ph/ (LTMS Driver's Manual > Licensing Information)
Last checked: 2026-10-26

The manual notes: in case of conflict between the manual and the LTO Citizen's Charter, the
Citizen's Charter prevails.

## Student-Driver's Permit (SP): qualifications and requirements

- Filipinos at least 16 years old.
  - PSA/Local Civil Registry authenticated Certificate of Birth, or Passport.
  - Married women: also PSA Certificate of Marriage.
  - Applicants below 18: parent's or guardian's consent, with any valid government-issued ID
    (with photo and signature).
- Foreigners at least 18 years old who have been in the Philippines for at least one (1) month,
  with proof of stay of at least six (6) months: Passport and visa valid for at least six
  months, or PSA/Local Civil Registry authenticated Certificate of Birth if born in the
  Philippines.
- Physically and mentally fit to drive: Medical Certificate from an accredited medical clinic.
- Able to read and write in Filipino, English or any major dialect.
- No unsettled traffic violation.
- Completed a 15-hour Theoretical Driving Course (TDC).
- Other requirements: TIN, if employed; duly accomplished APL Form (Application for Permits and
  Licenses).

Validity of the SP:
- Valid for one (1) year from issuance. After one year it can no longer be used for the
  practical driving course, but it can still be used to apply for a non-professional driver's
  license if the practical driving course was completed within that one year.
- After two (2) years from issuance, the holder must get a new SP (same SP number) and undergo
  the apprenticeship period again from the new issue date. Training records are kept.
- The SP may be renewed on or before its expiry date if the holder is not yet ready to apply
  for a driver's license.

## New non-professional driver's license (codes A, A1, B, B1, B2)

- At least 17 years old, with a valid SP. If 17, parent's or guardian's consent (with valid
  government ID with photo and signature). Foreigners: at least 18 years old.
- Able to read and write.
- SP holder for at least one (1) month.
- Attended and passed the Practical Driving Course (PDC) from an LTO-accredited licensed
  driver, LTO Driver's Education Center, LTO-accredited driving school, or LTO-accredited TESDA
  training center.
- Physically and mentally fit to drive: Medical Certificate from an accredited medical clinic.
- No unsettled traffic violation.
- Passed the automated theoretical examination.
- Passed the practical driving test.
- Other requirements: TIN, if employed; duly accomplished APL Form.

Validity: a new driver's license is valid for five (5) years, counted from the licensee's date
of birth, unless revoked or suspended. A holder with no violations during the five years before
expiry is entitled to a renewal valid for ten (10) years.

## Renewing a driver's license

Requirements (non-professional and professional):
- Your current driver's license.
- Medical Certificate from an accredited medical clinic.
- No unsettled traffic violation.
- Completed the Driving Enhancement Program (DEP) before the initial renewal of a five-year
  license.
- Duly accomplished APL Form.
- If the license has not been renewed for more than two (2) years after expiry: pass the
  automated theoretical examination and the practical driving test, and pay the penalty on top
  of the basic fee.
- Professional licenses for foreigners: valid working visa.

Filipinos working or living abroad may renew online or through an authorized representative
with the necessary documents. On arrival in the Philippines they must appear at LTO for photo
capture, presentation of the Official Receipt, and submission of the medical certificate.

## Fees (from the manual)

Fees for a NEW permit or license:
- Student-Driver's Permit (new): application fee PHP 100.00 plus permit fee PHP 150.00, total
  PHP 250.00.
- Driver's or conductor's license (new): application fee PHP 100.00 plus license fee
  PHP 585.00, total PHP 685.00.

Fees for RENEWAL: the same application and permit/license fees as for new ones. If renewed on
time, only the license fee and one (1) computer fee are paid.

Additional PENALTY fees, only if renewing after expiry:
- Student-Driver's Permit expired 1 day up to 1 year: PHP 50.00 penalty. Expired more than 1
  year up to 2 years: PHP 100.00 penalty. Expired more than 2 years: PHP 150.00 penalty.
- Driver's or conductor's license expired 1 day up to 1 year: PHP 75.00 penalty. Expired more
  than 1 year up to 2 years: PHP 150.00 penalty. Expired more than 2 years: PHP 225.00 penalty.
- License expired 1 day to 2 years: pay the license fee, the penalty, and one (1) computer fee.
- Re-examinees: pay the application fee, license fee, penalty, and two (2) computer fees.
- The computer fee amount is not stated in this part of the manual.
